Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Tranpages

We support PDF, DOCX, TXT, HTML, scanned images (via OCR), audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A), and video files (MP4). Our AI engine extracts text from any format while preserving the original document structure.
Tranpages analyzes your document's visual layout — logos, colors, tables, headers, footers, signatures, and fonts — then reconstructs the translated version with identical formatting. Only the text changes; everything else stays pixel-perfect.
We support over 100 languages including all major world languages, regional languages, and many African, Asian, and indigenous languages. Our AI translation engine handles language-specific nuances like right-to-left scripts, character-based writing systems, and tonal languages.
Absolutely. All documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. We are GDPR compliant and offer data residency options (EU, US, Asia-Pacific, Africa). Enterprise plans include zero-retention mode, customer-managed encryption keys, and HIPAA compliance.
Yes! Our batch processing feature lets you translate a single document into multiple languages simultaneously, or translate multiple documents into one target language. This is available on Pro plans and above.
We use state-of-the-art AI models that understand context, industry terminology, and cultural nuances. Our translations typically achieve 95-98% accuracy. You can further improve results by using our translation glossary feature to define preferred terms for your organization.
Free plans support files up to 10 pages. Pro plans handle documents up to 100 pages, and Business/Enterprise plans have no page limits. Individual file uploads can be up to 50MB. For larger files, contact our enterprise team.
Yes! Business plans include team workspaces with 5 seats, shared translation memories, collaborative glossaries, and comment threads on translated documents. Enterprise plans offer unlimited seats with role-based access controls.
Yes. Our REST API allows you to integrate Tranpages directly into your applications, CMS, or document workflows. API access is available on Business and Enterprise plans with comprehensive documentation, SDKs for Python, Node.js, and Java, and webhook support.
